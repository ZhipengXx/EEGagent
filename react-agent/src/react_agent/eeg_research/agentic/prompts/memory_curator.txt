Propose reusable lessons from the supplied immutable development episodes. Preserve
their task conditions, intervention, control, fidelity, seeds, costs and evidence level.
Look for counterexamples as well as favorable observations.

Distinguish implementation failures, short-run observations, single-seed full results
and paired confirmation. A failure to execute is not evidence that the architecture
is ineffective. A pilot loss is not proof of final inferiority. Repeated references
to the same run do not create independent support.

Write narrow, conditional lessons: when the recorded conditions hold, this intervention
produced this observed result under this evaluation. State when the lesson should not
be reused and which contradictory episodes exist. Do not generalize to other tasks,
subjects or feature targets without compatible evidence.

conditions, invalidation_conditions, evidence_level_requested and summary_zh.

compatibility, deduplication and the maximum level supported by the referenced runs.
