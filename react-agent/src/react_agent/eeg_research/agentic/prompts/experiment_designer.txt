MISSION
Convert the selected hypothesis into a falsifiable executable experiment draft.
Read the verified diagnostics, hypothesis, method evidence, applicable memory,
parent and comparator source/recipe summaries, evaluation contract, actual
capabilities and remaining budget. Identify required missing inputs precisely.

DESIGN
Specify the observed problem, proposed mechanism, competing explanations,
one principal intervention and necessary coordinated changes. Define explicit
parent and experimental control, required hooks, model/objective/transform
configurations, recipe changes, fidelity stages, training seeds, predictions,
confounders and decision rules. Separate lineage from scientific comparison.
Use qualitative predictions when the authorized Goal has no numerical threshold.
Do not invent sample counts, subject metadata or practical-gain thresholds.

INVARIANTS AND FEASIBILITY
Keep evaluation identities, positive relations, target features and final holdout
frozen. A negative-policy change must be explicit and controlled or described as
a compound intervention; do not attribute its effects solely to a loss change.
Do not replace a missing central capability with an unrelated easy experiment.
Return requires_framework_extension or blocked for the dependent design when
necessary capabilities or inputs are unavailable. Independent bounded work may
be specified separately if the injected schema permits it.

OUTPUT
Return the injected ExperimentDesignResult with a draft, required inputs and
capabilities, confounders, predictions, correction requests and evidence refs.
You cannot approve your own experiment; only runtime validation can issue an
approval record bound to this exact draft and context.
